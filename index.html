Core Trigonometric Definitions & Oblique Laws

Right Triangles & Unit Circle

sin θ = Opp / Hyp | cos θ = Adj / Hyp | tan θ = Opp / Adj

Unit Circle coordinates: (cos θ, sin θ) on radius r = 1.

Oblique Triangles (No Right Angle)

Law of Sines: \(\frac{a}{\sin A} = \frac{b}{\sin B} = \frac{c}{\sin C}\)

Law of Cosines: \(c^2 = a^2 + b^2 - 2ab \cos C\)

Step-by-Step Problem Solving Examples (Right & Oblique Triangles)

Example 1: Pythagorean Theorem

Problem: In a right triangle, leg a = 6 and leg b = 8. Find hypotenuse c.

Solution: c² = 6² + 8² = 36 + 64 = 100 → c = 10

Example 2: Law of Sines

Problem: In ΔABC, angle A = 45°, angle B = 60°, and side a = 10. Find side b.

Solution: b / sin(60°) = 10 / sin(45°) → b = 10 × (0.8660 / 0.7071) = 12.25

Example 3: Law of Cosines

Problem: In ΔABC, side a = 7, side b = 10, and included angle C = 60°. Find side c.

Solution: c² = 7² + 10² - 2(7)(10)cos(60°) = 49 + 100 - 140(0.5) = 149 - 70 = 79 → c = 8.89
