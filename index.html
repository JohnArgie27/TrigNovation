The Circular Functions & Pythagorean Identity

Let $(x, y)$ be the point on the unit circle corresponding to angle $\theta$. The primary circular functions are defined as $\cos(\theta) = x$, $\sin(\theta) = y$, and $\tan(\theta) = \frac{y}{x}$. The fundamental Pythagorean identity states that $\sin^2(\theta) + \cos^2(\theta) = 1$.
